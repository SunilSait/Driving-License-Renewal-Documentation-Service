Service 03

RC Renewal (Registration Certificate)

Your vehicle's RC must be renewed every 5 years after the initial 15-year period. Driving with an expired RC is a punishable offence. We send timely reminders and handle the full renewal process.

  • RC renewal for two-wheelers, cars, and commercial vehicles
  • PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate coordination
  • Vehicle fitness inspection liaison
  • Tax payment assistance and road tax filing
  • Updated smart card RC delivery

Service 04

International Driving Permit (IDP)

Planning to drive in a foreign country? An International Driving Permit (IDP) is a United Nations-issued document that serves as a certified translation of your Indian license. Valid in 185+ countries.

  • IDP application assistance and form preparation
  • Valid for 1 year from date of issue
  • Recognized in 185+ countries
  • Fast-tracked 2-day processing available
  • Must be carried alongside original Indian DL
