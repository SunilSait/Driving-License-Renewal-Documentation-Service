Driving License Renewal

Documents required for renewal of expired or expiring DL for all vehicle classes

Identity & License Documents

  • Original Driving License (expiring/expired)
  • Aadhaar Card (original + photocopy)
  • PAN Card or Voter ID (identity proof)
  • Passport-size photographs (2 nos.)

Address Proof (any one)

  • Aadhaar Card with current address
  • Passport (valid)
  • Electricity / Water / Gas bill (within 3 months)
  • Bank passbook with address

Medical & Forms

  • Form 9 — Application for Renewal of DL
  • Form 1 — Medical fitness self-declaration
  • Form 1-A — Doctor-attested (for LMV-TR, HMV)
  • Filled application form (we assist)

Additional Notes

  • If expired >5 years, fresh driving test may apply
  • Biometric (fingerprint + photo) at RTO may be required
  • Online payment of government fee required
  • We handle all RTO appointment scheduling

Address Change on DL

Documents required to update your residential address on your driving license

Core Documents

  • Original Driving License (to be updated)
  • Aadhaar Card with new address
  • Passport-size photographs (2 nos.)

New Address Proof (any two)

  • Aadhaar with new address
  • Recent utility bill (within 3 months)
  • Rental agreement (notarized)
  • Passport with new address

Forms & Applications

  • Form 33 — Application for change of address
  • Form 1 — Medical fitness self-declaration

Important Notes

  • Both old and new state changes handled
  • Processing time: 3–5 working days
  • NOC may be required for inter-state change

RC Renewal

Documents required for registration certificate renewal for all vehicle types

Vehicle Documents

  • Original RC Book
  • Valid Motor Insurance Certificate
  • Valid PUC Certificate
  • Fitness Certificate (for commercial vehicles)

Owner's Documents

  • Owner's Aadhaar Card
  • Driving License of owner
  • Passport-size photograph (1 no.)

Forms Required

  • Form 25 — Application for RC Renewal
  • Road tax payment receipt (if applicable)

Notes for Commercial

  • Route permit required for goods vehicles
  • Load test certificate for overloaded permits
  • We arrange fitness inspection liaison

International Driving Permit

Documents required to obtain an International Driving Permit (IDP) for driving abroad

Identity Documents

  • Valid Original Indian Driving License
  • Valid Passport (with at least 1 year validity)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card

Travel Documents

  • Valid Visa (destination country)
  • Flight ticket / Travel itinerary
  • Proof of travel date (booking confirmation)

Photographs & Forms

  • Passport-size photographs (4 nos.)
  • Form 4-A — IDP Application Form
  • Self-attested copies of all documents

Key Information

  • Valid for 1 year from date of issue
  • Recognized in 185+ countries
  • Must carry original Indian DL along with IDP
  • Express 48-hour processing available
Pro Tips

Before You Submit — Important Reminders

Always Self-Attest Copies

Sign across every photocopy with "True Copy — Self Attested" along with your full name and date.

Use Recent Photos

Photographs should be taken within the last 6 months on a white background, without glasses.

Don't Wait for Expiry

Renew your DL or RC up to 1 year before expiry to avoid fines. We send automated reminders to our clients.
